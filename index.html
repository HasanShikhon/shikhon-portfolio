Hero case study

The OVC that out-distributed the competition

Brand Master 3.0 — a national UIUMF competition, 2023.

Brand Master 3.02023
Watch the OVC on YouTube ↗
Competition entry · Education consultancy trust gap · Co-produced & personally distributed
Challenge
Bangladesh's education consultancy sector has a trust problem — students don't know which consultancies to believe.
Insight
A concept only works if people actually watch it. Distribution decides what "performs," not the pitch deck.
Idea
Co-produce an OVC that speaks directly to that trust gap as the centerpiece of a national campaign entry.
Execution
Helped produce the video as part of a team, then personally led its distribution and audience outreach.
Outcome
Earned the second-highest view count of all competing entries on a view-based leaderboard. Team advanced to the finals — top 8 nationally.
Why it matters for Personal Care: personal care brands live or die on trust and attention. This is direct, evidenced proof of both — not a case study I read about.

O-POTATO
Founding builder · Top 11, CSE471 Project Summit
Opportunity
Local restaurants coordinate manually — customers have no unified way to see live offers or book a seat in real time.
Insight
Diners act on what they can see right now. The friction was visibility, not demand.
Execution
Built a two-sided app in Flutter and Firebase — restaurant owners push live pricing, availability and promotions instantly; diners follow restaurants through a real-time social feed.
What I learned
Consumers act on what's visible and current right now — the same principle behind why a brand's offers need to reach people in real time, not just exist somewhere.
